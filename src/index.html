About Groove Music Pass

Microsoft Corporation • Windows Music Streaming Service

The History of Groove Music Pass

Groove Music (formerly Xbox Music and Zune Music) was Microsoft's digital music streaming and download service integrated directly into Windows 10, Xbox One, Windows Phone, iOS, and Android.

With a Groove Music Pass, subscribers could stream tens of millions of songs, download offline tracks, create radio stations based on artists, and sync their personal music collection via OneDrive.

The 2017 Sunset Announcement

On October 2, 2017, Microsoft announced that the Groove Music Pass streaming service would be discontinued on December 31, 2017.

Microsoft partnered closely with Spotify to allow Groove Music subscribers to seamlessly move their music collection, custom playlists, and favorite albums to Spotify with just a few clicks inside the Windows 10 app.

Groove Desktop Application Features

  • Native Windows Player: High-performance local audio playback for MP3, FLAC, WAV, M4A, and AAC files.
  • Curated Groove Playlists: Built-in nostalgic playlists (Top Hits 2017, Rock Classics, Chill Vibe, Synthwave Essentials).
  • Spotify Migration Tool: Full support for exporting Groove playlists to CSV or Spotify search links.
  • Fluent Dark & Light Design: Modern Windows UI matching Microsoft's original Groove design aesthetics.
